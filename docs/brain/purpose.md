# Purpose — why Math Tracker exists

## Mission
Revolutionize education by proving that quality beats bulk. The enemy is the
60-student classroom; Aristora classrooms hold **10 students maximum**. The promise
to every student who joins: **an A result within a predicted time**, through
personalized education — and when a student is weak, Aristora has a solution for
that too. Students today fail despite attending multiple classes and schools
because of bulk classes, zero individual attention, and Gen-Z memory/retention
problems. Aristora exists to fix exactly that.

This app is the scaling engine: small classes are hard to scale (big
infrastructure, big teacher pool), and Math Tracker is what makes scaling them
possible — toward a billion-dollar education enterprise.

## Who it serves
- **Users of the app:** teachers and admins ONLY. Never students or parents
  directly. (Today: the founder is the sole user.)
- **Scale today:** ~40 students running through the system.

## Business model
- **Today:** the founder's personal classes, priced per hour, day to day.
- **In 2 years:** become like a school — multiple centers or franchising,
  whichever proves most profitable. The exact path is deliberately undecided;
  profitability will choose it.

## What success looks like (1–2 years)
Success is **brand, not counts** — become the Apple of the education industry:
when anyone wants quality education, they come to Aristora by default. The
concrete win condition: **more students than any tuition center in the area.**

## Non-negotiables
- **Class size NEVER goes above 10.** When scaling pressure comes, the answer is
  more rooms and more teachers — never bigger classes.

## Priority compass
The **learning engine** matters most. It IS the moat — the algorithm that
guarantees an A result within predicted time. When choosing what to improve,
favor the learning engine first; everything else serves it.
